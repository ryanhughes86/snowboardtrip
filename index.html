Mayrhofen Station Accommodations (Budget Focus)

Staying within 8 minutes of Mayrhofen Bahnhof is the sweet spot: step off the Innsbruck/Salzburg train, hop straight on the free Green Line ski bus to Hintertux Glacier, and stay seconds from Spar and Billa supermarkets.

Betterpark Hintertux + Mount Penken

Why this beats France or Salzburg in mid-December: you get permanent autumn-built glacier kickers at Hintertux plus local tree runs in Mayrhofen.

Zillertal Superskipass: ~200 km Open Terrain

Betterpark Hintertux (Glacier)

3,200m Peak

The park is built right onto glacier ice and is fully operating long before mid-December.

  • Setup: Pro jump line, medium kicker line, easy line, and large multi-tier jib/rail racks.
  • Dedicated Lift: Olperer drag lifts lap directly through the park so you don't waste time riding down.
  • Logistics: Take the free Green Line ski bus from Mayrhofen Bahnhof (35–40 mins scenic valley drive).

PenkenPark & Local Runs

Local Mountain

The week before Christmas, Mayrhofen's valley lifts (Penkenbahn & Horbergbahn) open for winter operations.

  • Runs: 142 km of pistes, wide tree-lined carving groomers, and the infamous 78% steep *Harakiri*.
  • Park Setup: Early-season medium jump, beginner tables, and rainbow boxes right off the Sun-Jet chair.
  • Zero Commute: On bad weather/flat light days, ride Penken without taking the bus to the glacier.

Why Alternative Destinations Were Excluded

Morzine / Avoriaz

Town elevation is low (1,000m). Snow down in town is hit-or-miss mid-Dec. Avoriaz park is only small beginner rails with no full jump lines built yet.

Tignes (France)

High elevation ensures snow, but Geneva airport transfers run £130–£160+ and resort food is significantly higher than Austria, pushing costs to £1,300+.

Salzburg Resorts (Flachau/Saalbach)

Low base altitudes (800–1,000m). Even if cruising pistes open via cannons, major parks (*Absolut Park*) don't finish full jump builds until late December.
